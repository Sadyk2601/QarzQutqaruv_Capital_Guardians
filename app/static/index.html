The problem

MKBANK's problem loans grew from 4.4% to 9.9% of its portfolio in seven months (1 Jan → 1 Aug 2026, 2.28 trillion UZS; Central Bank of Uzbekistan). Banks score borrowers when they give a loan and call them after a missed payment, but the months in between, when a small business can still be saved, are not watched. In our test data, 48% of next month's defaulters had not missed a payment yet.

Step 1 · Model 1
Who is in trouble
LightGBM with monotone constraints (more delay can never lower risk), trained on 22,384 real anonymised borrowers (public UCI data). Features compare each borrower with their own history. ROC-AUC 0.765 on an untouched hold-out.
Step 2 · Model 2
What to do
For each allowed action (SMS, personal SMS, call, guarantor call, letter, visit, rescue offer) it estimates the loss prevented minus the cost, then spends the officers' minutes where they save the most. It starts from published field experiments and learns from real outcomes.
Step 3 · Rescue plan
How to save the loan
"Path back to green": the smallest next payment that lowers the risk. Optimized plan: the smallest term extension with a season-shaped schedule, payment ≤ 30% of income, present value unchanged for the bank. Offer letter in EN / RU / UZ.

What is new compared with a standard early-warning system

  • Standard systems stop at a watchlist. We also choose the action, plan the officer's day, and give a rescue plan that is affordable for the borrower and neutral for the bank.
  • Own-norm signals work for micro-borrowers with no financial statements; card and app activity act as early signals.
  • The system learns which action works for which problem in Uzbekistan (pilot with a 10% control group).

Trust and deployment

  • A person decides; hard steps go to the credit committee. Every score, decision and outcome is in a SHA-256 hash-chained audit log.
  • Customer data never leaves the bank: one on-prem server, no external API, model files of a few hundred KB, letters from templates.
  • The model does not use sex or age; we check fairness across groups. Thresholds per segment are set by the risk department.

Pilot and business case assumptions stated in the deck

  • 3-month shadow pilot in one branch: the system scores daily, decisions don't change, then we compare with what really happened.
  • Pilot ≈ 208M UZS; bank-wide run ≈ 422M UZS a year; one score ≈ 0.3 UZS.
  • Preventing just 1% of MKBANK's new problem loans keeps ≈ 10.7bn UZS a year. Break-even is 0.06%, about 28 average loans a year.
  • Next: retrain on MKBANK data, KATM credit-bureau "debt carousel" signals, a borrower self-check app, a rate-shock stress test for the Central Bank, other banks and 134 microfinance organisations.
