Known Limitations

  • New loans (<6 months history) are rejected to ensure reliable baseline.
  • Sudden shocks (illness, fire) cannot be predicted by past signals.
  • Model trained on Taiwan data; requires retraining for pilot.
  • Seasonal business drops might look like defaults without 12+ months history.
  • Intentional non-payers with perfect prior history show no signals.
